VICReg in one picture. Three forces, three collapses denied. Invariance snaps a sample's two views together — but on its own it crushes every embedding to one point. Variance floors the spread of each coordinate, ruling the point out; yet a single tilted line still satisfies it, hiding all information in one dimension. Covariance zeros the cross-correlations, untangling that line into a round cloud — the isotropic Gaussian SIGReg later certifies.
